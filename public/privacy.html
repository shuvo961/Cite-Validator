Privacy Policy

Cite Validator is built to be transparent about reference checking. This policy explains what the MVP stores and what may be sent to metadata services.

What you paste

References you validate may be stored in local app history so reports can be exported and reopened. When you use trusted source lookup, citation metadata may be queried against services such as Crossref, DOI.org, OpenAlex, PubMed, Europe PMC, DataCite, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Google Books, Open Library, ISBN, and ISSN-related services.

Accounts

If you log in, the app stores your email, name, provider, session, and validation history. Public login uses Google OAuth when configured by the site owner.

Local MVP storage

The local version stores data in SQLite under the project data folder. Do not upload local database files to GitHub.

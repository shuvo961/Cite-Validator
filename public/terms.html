Terms of Use

Cite Validator helps identify citation risk, but it does not replace editorial, legal, or academic judgment.

Free academic tool

Cite Validator is provided as a free tool for academic reference checking, citation conversion, and hallucinated citation detection.

No guarantee of perfect verification

The app checks trusted metadata sources and flags uncertainty. If a result is uncertain, unverifiable, suspicious, or likely fabricated, users should manually verify the source before relying on it.

Responsible use

Do not use the app to overload public metadata providers, submit unlawful content, or misrepresent verification results.

Exports

Exported CSV, PDF, DOC, BibTeX, and RIS reports are generated from available metadata and the user-provided input.

Supported formats

Paste almost any bibliography text

Cite Validator supports common styles, identifier-only lists, and messy copied references from PDFs. The app detects citation style automatically when possible and shows a split preview before validation.

Try a pasted list Check a DOI
Aa

Academic styles

APA, IEEE, MLA, Chicago, Vancouver, ACS, Harvard, numbered styles, and journal-specific references.

ID

Identifiers

DOI, ISBN, ISSN, PMID, PMCID, arXiv ID, URLs, publisher landing pages, and book metadata.

TXT

Imports

Plain text, copied bibliography sections, BibTeX, RIS, CSV-style lists, and DOI-only batches.

PDF

Messy PDF text

Broken lines, page numbers, hyphenation, DOI URLs split across lines, and pasted-together references.
